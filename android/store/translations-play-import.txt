App name:
NEXBILL: PS Rental Cashier

Short description:
PS rental billing on your phone: timers, F&B cashier, auto TV control, reports.

Full description:
NEXBILL is a billing and cashier app for PS and game-console rental businesses. Booth timers, F&B cashier, automatic TV on/off, bookings, and owner reports — all from an Android phone, no cashier computer needed.

⏱️ PS RENTAL BILLING FROM YOUR PHONE
• Start, pause, extend, move, and stop sessions right from the phone
• Hour packages, per-console rates, promos, and membership with points
• Booking Map: every booth's schedule by the hour on one screen
• Automatic pop-up for every new online booking, so the cashier never misses one

🛒 CASHIER & STOCK
• F&B cashier on the same bill as the rental
• Scan product barcodes and member-card QR codes with the phone camera
• Stock, stock-take, and goods received with barcodes
• One-tap receipts to 58/80mm Bluetooth thermal printers

📺 AUTOMATIC TV & POWER
• TVs on WiFi smart plugs (Tuya / Tasmota) switch on and off with each session
• Android TV / Google TV controlled through the NEXBILL Relay Agent — on a PC or a spare Android phone
• Time-left warnings and a "Time's up" screen on the TV

🔔 ALERTS EVEN WHEN THE APP IS CLOSED
• Booth sessions ending or ended
• Food orders, extra-time requests, and cashier calls from the booth customer QR
• New online bookings, low stock, and the revenue summary when a shift closes

📊 FOR OWNERS
• Real-time revenue, shift, and cash reports from anywhere, with shift history by day/month/year
• Automatic accounting: balance sheet, profit & loss, payables, assets, and bill-payment (PPOB) records
• Cashier anti-fraud, multi-branch, and AI Business Assistant (Pro plan)
• Role-based access: owner, manager, supervisor, cashier, kitchen, accountant
