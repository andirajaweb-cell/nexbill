Salin ke Play Console → **Grow → Kehadiran di Play Store → Listingan Play Store utama**.

Hindari kata "PlayStation"/logo Sony di nama, ikon, dan grafis (merek pihak lain) — cukup "rental PS / konsol game".

```
NEXBILL is a billing and cashier app for PS and game-console rental businesses. All you need is an Android phone — no cashier computer required.

📱 CASHIER WITHOUT A PC
• Start, pause, extend, and stop rental sessions right from the phone
• F&B cashier on the same bill as the rental
• Scan product barcodes and member-card QR codes with the phone camera
• Online booking, membership & points, promos, and shift close

🧾 ONE-TAP BLUETOOTH RECEIPTS
• Print to 58/80mm thermal printers over Bluetooth, no print dialog
• Bluetooth Classic printers supported via the RawBT app

📺 AUTOMATIC TV CONTROL
• TVs on WiFi smart plugs switch on and off with each session
• Android TVs are controlled through the NEXBILL Relay Agent on an outlet device
• Time-left warnings and a "Time's up" screen on the TV

🔔 ALERTS EVEN WHEN THE APP IS CLOSED
• Booth sessions ending or ended
• F&B orders, extra-time requests, and cashier calls from the booth customer QR
• New online bookings and QRIS payments received
• Low stock, risky shifts, and the revenue summary when a shift closes (for owners)

📊 FOR OWNERS
• Real-time sales, shift, and cash reports from anywhere
```
